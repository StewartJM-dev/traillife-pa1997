The big events

Mark these now. Details and sign-ups go out in Trail Blazer Weekly and on Facebook.

Sunday, Sept 20

Trail Life Sunday

Trailmen in uniform at the morning and evening services at Living Word Baptist Church.

Late September

Court of Honor

Outdoor evening awards ceremony under the flags. Date announced soon.

Friday, Oct 30

Ultimate Manhunt: Find the Lost Patrol

A better Halloween alternative — tracking, teamwork, and radios in the dark. Hot dogs, campfire, and Dutch oven apple crisp at Living Word Baptist Church.

Saturday, Nov 14

Veterans Breakfast

Trailmen serve breakfast to the veterans of our community. RSVP by November 7.

Saturday, Dec 5

Iron Trail Chef & Christmas Party

Parent–Trailman Dutch oven showdown with one mystery ingredient, followed by the troop Christmas party.

Jan 29–31, 2027

Klondike Expedition

Navigators and Adventurers haul a boy-built sled through a winter skills challenge near Williamsport.
