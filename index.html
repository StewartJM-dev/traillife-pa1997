Every boy has a patrol

Trailmen advance by age through five patrols. Brothers of different ages meet on the same night, then break out with their own patrol.

  1. Ages 5–6

    Foxes

    First campfires, first knots, first flag ceremonies.

  2. Ages 7–8

    Hawks

    Branch pins in outdoor skills, heritage, science and more.

  3. Ages 9–11

    Mountain Lions

    Edge tools, fire building, tracking, the Forest Award.

  4. Ages 12–14

    Navigators

    Badges, campouts, and the beginnings of youth leadership.

  5. Ages 15–18

    Adventurers

    Lead the troop, plan the trek, earn the Freedom Award.
